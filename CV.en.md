## Experience

### Co-founder · Product Designer (UX/UI)
**MAITEEGO AI** · Remote · Since October 2024

_AI travel app (travel companion), B2C and mobile-first. In public beta for 18 months on iOS, Android and web; around 300 registered users. Sole designer; I work in a duo with the CTO and co-founder._

- I design the product end to end: **competitive analysis** (4 competitors, 7 criteria), **information architecture** and flows in Miro, wireframes, prototypes and a **design system in Figma** (typography, colour, spacing and reusable components).
- I designed the AI interaction so every answer is actionable: each place mentioned in the chat is a **verified card** that is added to the itinerary in one tap, with no copying or searching in another app.

- I evolved the product in beta with real users: a redesigned home (asking the AI as the main action), destination guides structured around the traveller's questions, a map with real-time information and an itinerary with weather, route and travel times between stops.
